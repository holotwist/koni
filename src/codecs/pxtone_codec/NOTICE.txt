PxTone Audio Codec Reimplementation for Koni (C Implementation)

The PxTone sound engine specification, synthesis algorithms, frequency tables,
and file formats (.ptcop, .pttune, .ptnoise, .ptvoice) were originally created
and designed by:

    Daisuke "Pixel" Amaya (Studio Pixel)
    https://studiopixel.jp/

This implementation in Koni is an independent, lightweight C reimplementation
inspired by and compatible with the original pxtone library.

Special thanks to Pixel for creating PxTone Collage, Cave Story (Doukutsu Monogatari),
and contributing this unique chiptune format to the community.
